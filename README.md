# Shield_Insurance_Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview  
This dashboard is created as part of my virtual internship with **AtliQ Technologies**. 
The goal is to help Shield Insurance make better decisions by turning raw data into actionable insights.


## 🏢 About the Company  
**Shield Insurance** provides flexible and affordable policies across major Indian cities like Mumbai, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Indore. 
They reach customers through various channels, including offline and online sales mode.



## ❓ Problem Statement  
The company wanted to leverage their data more effectively to make better data informed decisions. 
As a trial, they asked for a pilot dashboard that could offer useful insights into their data.



## 📊 Dashboard Sections  
The dashboard is created using Power BI and includes the following Pages:

1. **General Overview**  
   - Provides insights into the overall business performance of shield insurance.
   - City & Age-wise breakdown of performance, Monthly trends in revenue and customers, etc.

2. **Sales Mode Analysis**  
   - Helps understand which sales channel is performing best.
   - Revenue & customer distibution by sales modes, city wise performance of different sales modes, etc.  

4. **Age Group Analysis**
   - Useful for identifying key customer segments by age group.
   - Distribution of policies, customers and revenue by different age group  



## 💡 Insights & Recommendations

### 🔹 Insight 1  
**Direct Agents generate the most revenue and Online modes are seeing a steady growth**  
✅ *Recommendation*: Focus on improving the app experience and invest in digital marketing to attract more customers.

### 🔹 Insight 2  
**The 31–45 age group contributed highest to the total revenue and 17-24 age group contributed least**  
✅ *Recommendation*: Introduce targeted plans & offers for 31-45 age group to boost retention and for 17-24 age group, run awareness programs through social media platforms.

### 🔹 Insight 3  
**Delhi-NCR has the highest number of customers but Indore lags behind**  
✅ *Recommendation*: Run loyalty programs in Delhi NCR to keep this momentum going, run marketing programs in Indore and highlight best selling policies to attract more customers.
